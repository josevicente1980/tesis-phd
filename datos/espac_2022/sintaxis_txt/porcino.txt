*                            CONSTRUCCIÓN DE LA TABLA 10                                    *

WEIGHT BY fact_exp_fin.
* Tablas personalizadas.
CTABLES
  /VLABELS VARIABLES=gp_tn gp_trs gp_trc gp_tro  ual_prov DISPLAY=NONE  /VLABELS 
    VARIABLES=gp_k901 DISPLAY=LABEL
  /TABLE gp_tn + gp_trs + gp_trc + gp_tro  + ual_prov BY gp_k901 [SUM]
  /SLABELS VISIBLE=NO
  /CATEGORIES VARIABLES=gp_tn gp_trs gp_trc gp_tro ual_prov ORDER=A KEY=VALUE EMPTY=INCLUDE.    

WEIGHT OFF. 
FILTER OFF. 
USE ALL. 
EXECUTE.
